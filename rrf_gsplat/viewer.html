The camera starts at the held-out view’s receiver pose with the dataset’s own camera (90°×67.4°), so at the pose the render should match the released model’s prediction beside it; once you move away there is no ground truth, and the render shows what the field looks like, not whether it is right. Each file is 240 MB and streams on demand. The renderer is fetched from a CDN, so this tab alone needs the network.
